FAQ

Common planning questions.

Which locations do you currently serve?

Grindmine primarily serves Mumbai and nearby regions, with selected outstation projects based on scope and schedule.

Can you handle both small and large events?

Yes. We tailor team size, service configuration, and menu structure to your guest count and format.

Do you provide retail coffee delivery?

No. Grindmine is a specialty coffee experience company focused on live event service and premium coffee setups.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend 2–4 weeks for most events, and earlier for custom or high-capacity formats.

Do you customize coffee menus for different events?

Yes. We curate beverage menus based on event type, audience preferences, and overall guest experience.

Do you provide baristas and service staff?

Absolutely. Grindmine provides trained baristas, service professionals, and operational support for smooth event execution.

Can your setup work for indoor and outdoor venues?

Yes. Our coffee bar setups adapt to indoor venues, outdoor environments, weddings, corporate spaces, festivals, and popup formats.
